### Metadata-curated Cheese Database

![MetaCheeseDB](assets/gitbook/images/metacheesedb.png)

[**MetaCheeseDB**](https://magliulo.github.io/raffaelemagliulo/files/MetaCheeseDB.html) is an open source metadata-curated catalogue of cheese metagenomes for studying the cheese microbiome across 18 harmonised metadata, including production technologies, EU-labelling, and country of origin. This database indeed integrates **1,593** shotgun metagenomes spanning **156 cheese subtypes** from **19 countries** between **4 continents**, combining publicly available datasets with newly sequenced **Protected Designation of Origin (PDO) and Protected Geographical Indication (PGI)** cheeses. MetaCheeseDB pairs high-resolution microbiome data with **cheese-specific metadata** so that researchers can test ecological and functional hypotheses at scale, and stakeholders can explore microbiome signatures relevant to quality, authenticity, and technological innovation.

The browsing interface of MetaCheeseDB can be explored [here](https://magliulo.github.io/raffaelemagliulo/files/MetaCheeseDB.html), whilst the rationale behind the metadata harmonisation is stored at [this link](https://magliulo.github.io/metacheesedb/jekyll/2025-12-03-metadata.html).
